## Featured projects

### [VK-Tech-case](https://github.com/M00NFACED/VK-Tech-case)

Hybrid **Dense + Sparse search** over corporate messenger messages for the VK WorkSpace case at the Samsung Innovation Campus hackathon.

- Thread-aware chunking to keep conversations intact
- Separate renderers for dense embeddings and BM25-friendly sparse text
- Multi-query retrieval with HyDE and RRF
- Metadata and entity enrichment for sparse queries

**Result:** **+11.8%** to baseline (Score 0.4682 → 0.5235); **14th overall in Russia**, **1st among VSU teams**.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Qdrant` `BM25` `HyDE` `RRF`

---

### [YOLOv8 + Generative Augmentation](https://github.com/M00NFACED/yolov8-generative-augmentation)

Generative data augmentation pipeline for small object-detection datasets. Objects are extracted from source images, new backgrounds are generated with **Stable Diffusion Inpainting**, and the results are composited into synthetic training data.

- 100 real images vs. 100 real + 900 synthetic images
- YOLOv8 fine-tuning with Ultralytics
- Bachelor's thesis project

**Result:** **+13.9% mAP@0.5** and **+21.2% mAP@0.5:0.95** over the baseline.

`Python` `PyTorch` `YOLOv8` `Diffusers` `Stable Diffusion` `OpenCV`
